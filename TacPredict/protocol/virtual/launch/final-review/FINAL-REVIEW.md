Catalogue never falls back to real trading when virtual config is missing. Old deposit-for-15-USDC welcome rewards and legacy promo redemption UI retired, no reward campaign implied. - Inactive monthly campaign foundation: capped immutable allowlists, domain-bound Merkle claims, start/end, per-wallet cap, unique month, pause/cancel and duplicate protection. Admin+AAL2 preview and optional atomic audit/draft save; additive v2 SQL draft with RLS, no public writes or balance editing. No $500 grant, campaign, root, reward budget, claims page or point formula activated. Existing versioned points/snapshot foundation remains separate.
- No CSS/theme/navigation redesign. Local before/after disconnected portfolio at 390x844 and 1280x900 has identical recorded component geometry, no page errors/overflow. This is **not** evidence of connected-wallet prediction-screen E2E. `visual-geometry.json` records measured scope; screenshots supplied separately with the handoff.

## Required new artifacts and approvals

A **new separate contract deployment** is required for feed pricing and monthly campaign controls. The existing canary is immutable, its 100 Virtual USDC grant cap was exhausted, and cannot be silently extended/upgraded into this design. New deployment requires explicit lifetime seed/grant caps, role holders, budgets and approval; no arbitrary individual balance editor is introduced.

Provider/formula: AggregatorV3 prototype, rational return/time-volatility/inventory model with explicit spread and amount impact. Full equations, source limitations, void/PnL semantics and operational costs are in PRICING.md. Commercial fast-feed/RPC costs and required new signing/report service permissions are unknown until selected; no invented cost or provider guarantee.

Environment and minimum permissions: see RUNBOOK.md (names only). Existing auth requires verified wallet ownership; admin preview requires admin+AAL2; service credentials stay server/indexer only. No new private key, sponsorship or secret is provisioned.
