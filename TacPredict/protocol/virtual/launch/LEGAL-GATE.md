# Legal / product disclosure gate — not legal clearance

The virtual-only, non-redeemable design reduces certain real-collateral risks; it is **not proof of exemption** from financial-market, gambling, competition/promotion, consumer, privacy, sanctions, tax or advertising law. User-paid native gas, future points/rewards, referral incentives and event types may affect analysis. No legal/compliance approval is claimed. No jurisdiction was chosen or restriction removed during this task.

Before public access, obtain jurisdiction-specific counsel review of:
- Legal operator identity, domicile/contact, age/capacity, targeted and excluded countries, actual product and any future redeemable benefits.
- Whether paid gas, prediction-event mechanics, prizes/points/referrals or marketing create regulated consideration, a promotion/competition or a regulated market. Do not advertise cash payouts, guaranteed winnings or a future TAC token.
- Existing restricted-jurisdiction text versus **actual server/edge enforcement**. Terms alone do not implement geofencing. Wallet location cannot be inferred reliably from an address. No production geo rules were configured or verified here.
- Privacy notice, wallet/IP/device data, public immutable transaction records, retention, abuse controls, processors and user rights; terms are not a substitute for a privacy policy. Avoid promising anonymity/deletion of blockchain records.
- Source-data licences, sports/team branding, fixture/result rights and provider terms. A key being configured does not establish licensed redistribution rights.
- Complaints, dispute windows, accessible settlement/incident support and fair, accurate disclosure of fees and finite liquidity.
